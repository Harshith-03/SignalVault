# Signal Vault

Every week, pharma launch teams are buried under hundreds of new studies, trial updates, FDA notices and news stories about their drug and its rivals. The AI tools that could help are often off-limits, because sensitive data can't leave the company. **Signal Vault** is a launch-intelligence assistant that runs entirely on your own laptop. It reads every piece of evidence about Tagrisso and Rybrevant and tells you what kind of signal it is, whether your team should act on it and who should own it. It also tracks how doctors, patients, payers and the media feel about efficacy, safety, convenience, cost and evidence quality over time. Every claim is backed by a word-for-word quote checked against the source. Uncertain calls go to a human review queue instead of being guessed. Private material like advisory-board transcripts is scrubbed of names before anyone sees it. Nothing leaves the vault, and we can prove it.

Built at HACK1984 for the **Signal-to-Dose** track (Within3). Also entered in **Privacy-First**, **Most Startup-able** and **Surge**.
