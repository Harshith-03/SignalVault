# Signal Dose Desk

**An offline launch-intelligence desk.** It reads public evidence on two EGFR+ lung-cancer drugs, Tagrisso and Rybrevant/Lazcluze. It tells a launch team which signals matter, why they matter and who should review them, and every claim is backed by a verbatim, verified quote. Everything runs on a local model, so nothing leaves the laptop.

Built at HACK1984 for the **Signal-to-Dose** track (Within3), with **Privacy-First** and **Most Startup-able** in mind.
