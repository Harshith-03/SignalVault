Q: How has first-line practice changed for EGFR-mutant patients over the past year?
A: More of my colleagues now offer amivantamab plus lazertinib to fit, younger patients with high-risk features. The MARIPOSA overall survival data convinced a lot of people. For older or frailer patients, osimertinib monotherapy is still my default; it is very well tolerated.
Q: How do you weigh the evidence behind each option?
A: FLAURA2 and MARIPOSA are both large randomised phase 3 trials, so the evidence is strong. What I'd like is head-to-head data; cross-trial comparisons are shaky.

A: Yes. I personally treated a 58-year-old man, a retired teacher, who started amivantamab and lazertinib in August. He recovered and is back on treatment with apixaban.

Q: And convenience?
A: The subcutaneous formulation is a big improvement; infusion reactions were the main complaint. Tagrisso is still the easiest: one tablet, no chair time.
